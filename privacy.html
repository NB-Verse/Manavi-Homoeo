Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

This policy explains how Manasvi Homoeo & Wellness Studio handles the information you share through this website (aalok.co.in).

Who we are

Manasvi Homoeo & Wellness Studio, First floor, Alok Medical and Homeo Pharmacy, Juna Dawa Bazar, near Swasthya Hospital, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra 414001. Phone: +91 90671 97969. Email: manasvihomeo25@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you request an appointment: your name, mobile number, preferred day and time, and visit type. A short reason for the visit and your email address are optional.

When you send feedback: your name, rating and message.

We do not ask for medical reports or a detailed medical history through this website. Please share those with the doctor at your consultation.

How your request reaches us

If you choose WhatsApp or email, your phone opens that app with the message ready. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the message is then handled under WhatsApp's or your email provider's terms.

If you choose "Send to the clinic", your details are submitted through our website host, Netlify, and passed to the clinic.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about your appointment or to reply to your feedback. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Health tools

The BMI, due date and period tools work entirely on your device. What you enter in them is not sent to us or stored by this website.

Stored on your device

The website remembers your language choice (English or Marathi) in your browser. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the map is provided by Google Maps. These services may receive your device's IP address. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Google Calendar and the clinic's online listing open those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep appointment and feedback messages only as long as needed to arrange your care and follow-up, or as required by law.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, call +91 90671 97969 or email manasvihomeo25@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
