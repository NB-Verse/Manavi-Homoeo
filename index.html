About the clinic

Manasvi Homoeo & Wellness Studio

A homoeopathy and wellness clinic on the first floor of Alok Medical and Homeo Pharmacy in Juna Dawa Bazar, Ahilyanagar. Patients are seen by Dr. Vrushali Manojkumar Bafna, who uses homoeopathy together with naturopathy and yoga therapy where it suits the patient.

Homoeopathy

Medicines chosen after a detailed history of your symptoms, routine and past health.

  • For recent and long-standing complaints
  • For patients of all ages
  • Follow-up visits to review progress

Naturopathy

Guidance on food, daily routine and natural therapies to support your treatment.

  • Diet and routine advice
  • Support for digestion, weight and lifestyle habits
  • Simple changes you can follow at home

Yoga therapy

Guided yoga, breathing and relaxation planned around your health needs.

  • For stress, sleep, weight and digestion
  • Practices for women's health
  • Taught at a pace that suits you

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need an appointment?

    Requesting a time in advance helps the doctor give you enough time. Call the clinic to check whether you can come without an appointment.

    What should I bring to the first visit?

    Old prescriptions, test reports and scans, and a list of the medicines you take.

    Can I continue my other medicines?

    Do not stop any medicine on your own. Tell the doctor what you take, and she will advise you.

    Does the clinic offer naturopathy and yoga therapy?

    Yes. The doctor may suggest diet, routine or yoga therapy alongside homoeopathy where it suits you.

    Is online consultation available?

    Yes. Choose Online consultation when you request an appointment.

    Where exactly is the clinic?

    On the first floor of Alok Medical and Homeo Pharmacy, Juna Dawa Bazar, near Swasthya Hospital, Ahilyanagar 414001.

    How long does treatment take?

    It depends on the condition and how long you have had it. The doctor will discuss this with you after the first visit.

    How is my information used?

    Your details are used only to arrange your appointment. Read the privacy policy.
