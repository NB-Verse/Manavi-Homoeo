Diet and lifestyle guide

What to eat and what to avoid

Food, yoga and naturopathy pointers for common health problems and vitamin deficiencies. Search a condition, or a food such as "sugar" or "curd".

While you take homoeopathic medicine

  • Do not eat or drink anything, not even water, for 15 minutes before and 15 minutes after each dose.
  • Avoid raw onion, raw garlic and coffee for as long as you are taking the medicine.

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need an appointment?

    Requesting a time in advance helps the doctor give you enough time. Call the clinic to check whether you can come without an appointment.

    What should I bring to the first visit?

    Old prescriptions, test reports and scans, and a list of the medicines you take.

    Can I continue my other medicines?

    Do not stop any medicine on your own. Tell the doctor what you take, and she will advise you.

    Does the clinic offer naturopathy and yoga therapy?

    Yes. The doctor may suggest diet, routine or yoga therapy alongside homoeopathy where it suits you.

    Is online consultation available?

    Yes. Choose Online consultation when you request an appointment.

    Where exactly is the clinic?

    On the first floor of Shri Aalok Homoeo Pharmacy, Juna Dawa Bazar, near Swasthya Hospital, Ahilyanagar 414001.

    How long does treatment take?

    It depends on the condition and how long you have had it. The doctor will discuss this with you after the first visit.

    How should I take homoeopathic medicine?

    Do not eat or drink anything, not even water, for 15 minutes before and 15 minutes after each dose. While you are on homoeopathic medicine, avoid raw onion, raw garlic and coffee. Onion and garlic cooked in vegetables (sabji) and curries are fine.

    Can medicines be sent to other cities?

    Yes. The clinic packs and sends medicines to patients outside Ahilyanagar. Call or message the clinic to arrange it.

    How is my information used?

    Your details are used only to arrange your appointment. Read the privacy policy.
