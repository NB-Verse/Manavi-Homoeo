HOW TO ADD PHOTOS AND VIDEOS
============================
Put every photo and video in THIS "media" folder. The website finds them by file name and places
them automatically. Add, replace or delete files here and upload the folder again; nothing else to edit.

NAME THE FILES LIKE THIS (numbers start at 1 and go up without gaps)

Doctor's photo (top of the doctor section)
  doctor.jpg

Patient stories (shown in the "Patient feedback" section, as a swipeable row)
  feedback1.mp4, feedback2.mp4, feedback3.mp4 ...   patient video feedback
  image1.jpg, image2.png, image3.jpg ...             photos or screenshots of patient feedback

Gallery (shown under the doctor section, with tabs)
  clinic1.jpg, clinic2.jpg ...             the clinic, entrance, consultation room, yoga space
  achievement1.jpg, achievement2.jpg ...   awards, certificates, talks, camps
  packing1.jpg, packing2.mp4 ...           how medicines are packed and sent to other cities
  followup1.jpg, followup2.mp4 ...         how follow-up care works

Any of these can be a photo (.jpg .jpeg .png .webp) or a video (.mp4 .webm).
A tab or section appears only when it has at least one file.

CAPTIONS (optional): edit captions.txt
  One line per file:   feedback1.mp4 = Mrs. Patil, Savedi
YOUTUBE (optional, for long or heavy videos): edit youtube.txt
  One line per video:  category | YouTube link | caption
  Categories: feedback, clinic, achievement, packing, followup

KEEP THE SITE FAST
- Photos: under about 300 KB each (WhatsApp-sized photos are fine).
- Videos: under about 15 MB and under 1 minute. Put longer videos on YouTube and list them in youtube.txt.
- Use .mp4 for videos (iPhone .mov files do not play in all browsers; WhatsApp-forwarded videos are .mp4).

PERMISSION
- Show a patient's photo, message or video only with that patient's permission.
- Do not show reports, prescriptions or anything with another person's details.

VIDEO COVER PICTURE (optional): add a photo named like the video plus -poster, e.g. feedback1-poster.jpg.
It shows before the video plays (important on iPhones). Keep videos under about 10 MB each, or use YouTube.
